Tyrkland 'Turkland' Asia Ectors saga Ector A poorly defined region which encompasses Asia Minor and the Eurasian steppe.
Ertinborg Ertheneburg Europe Bærings saga Bæringr A now-ruined castle on the northern (Schleswig-Holstein) bank of the Elbe. The modern town of Artlenburg, whose name is derived from that of the old castle, stands on the opposite (Lower Saxony) side of the Elbe.
Bláland 'Blackland' Africa Klári saga Klárus An imprecise term for the parts of Africa bordering the Mediterranean and Red Sea.
